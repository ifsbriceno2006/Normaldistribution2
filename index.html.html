1️⃣ The Empirical Rule (68–95–99.7)

In a bell-shaped (normal) distribution, about 68% of values lie within 1 standard deviation (σ) of the mean (μ), 95% within 2σ, and 99.7% within 3σ. Pick a situation and move the sliders.

💡 Symmetry: the two tails share what is left over. Outside ±1σ is 100% − 68% = 32%, so each tail is 16%.

2️⃣ Reading the z-table

A z-score tells you how many standard deviations a value is from the mean: z = (x − μ) ÷ σ. The table entry is the area to the left of z, written as a decimal (e.g. 0.8508). Move z to see where the entry sits (row = z to 1 decimal, column = the 2nd decimal).
